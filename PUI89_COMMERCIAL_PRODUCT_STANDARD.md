# PUI89 Commercial Product & Company-Readiness Standard

**Portfolio rule:** mandatory for this and every future PUI89 project. Goal: lawful commercial use and an evidence-backed path to high-quality products and a future PUI89 company.

## Project-specific gates
AI motorcycle intelligence software and proposed rider-assistance product. Keep rider control authoritative; no autonomous-riding claims; validate fail-safe warnings, vibration/weather/EMC robustness, cybersecurity and qualified vehicle integration; obtain applicable road-vehicle approvals before commercial road use.

## Commercial rights and intellectual property
- The root LICENSE governs original materials the owner is entitled to license. MIT permits commercial use, modification and redistribution subject to its conditions.
- Third-party code, models/weights, datasets, images, CAD, fonts and other assets are not automatically covered. Record source, version, owner, exact licence, attribution, restrictions and commercial-use permission before inclusion or sale.
- Exclude assets with unclear, research-only, non-commercial, no-redistribution or incompatible terms. PUI89 trademarks are separate from software copyright.
- Maintain contributor permissions, copyright provenance, third-party notices and an SBOM for distributable software.

## First-class product quality
Define customer, intended use and exclusions; measurable requirements; architecture; reproducible tests; traceability; secure defaults; privacy/data governance; user and maintenance documentation; support, incident, rollback and end-of-life plans.

## Safety, security and regulatory gates
Complete hazard/risk assessment, safe-state design, rider override and misuse analysis before customer use. For AI, document data provenance, calibration, uncertainty, error rates and human review. Identify applicable vehicle, cybersecurity, functional-safety, privacy and road-approval requirements for each target market with qualified assessors. Simulation/software tests do not establish roadworthiness or physical safety.

## Commercial stage gates
| Gate | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| G0 Concept | Customer problem, intended use, exclusions, risks | Concept |
| G1 Requirements | Requirements, architecture, hazards, IP/data/licence review | Specified |
| G2 Prototype | Working software/prototype and reproducible tests | Prototype |
| G3 Verification | Tests, benchmarks, security review, failure analysis | Verified within scope |
| G4 Validation | Representative trials and independent review where needed | Validated within scope |
| G5 Compliance | Required legal, safety, privacy and market approvals | Approved only with evidence |
| G6 Pilot | Qualified supply, support, warranty and service processes | Pilot |
| G7 Commercial release | Sign-off, unit economics, quality controls and post-market support | Sale-ready only after required gates |

## Startup readiness
Maintain product catalogue, IP/contributor register, third-party licence inventory, customer discovery/pilot records, unit-economics model, supplier qualification, repair/warranty/support plans and separation of open-source code, proprietary components, services and brand assets. Existing open-source grants cannot simply be withdrawn.

## Strict zero-cost rule
Prefer free/open-source and offline/local tools. No paid APIs, subscriptions, cloud GPUs, paid datasets, metered services or unexpected billing. Check licence, provenance, security, resource fit and cost before adding dependencies/models/data/services. Record unfunded certification, lab testing, legal review or manufacturing as future funding/partner gates; do not incur charges without explicit approval.

## Required records
Create and maintain as the project matures: docs/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md, docs/HAZARD_AND_RISK_REGISTER.md, docs/VERIFICATION_AND_VALIDATION_PLAN.md, docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and docs/COMMERCIAL_READINESS.md. Their mention does not mean they exist or have passed review.

## Truthful claims
Use PROPOSED, IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, SIMULATION-ONLY, BENCH-TESTED, FIELD-TESTED, INDEPENDENTLY-VALIDATED and CERTIFIED/APPROVED only when supported by traceable evidence. Never market a concept as finished, autonomous or road-certified without evidence and approval.

**PUI89 standard:** commercial rights clarity, disciplined engineering, safety-first design, evidence before claims and zero-cost development until funding is explicitly approved.
